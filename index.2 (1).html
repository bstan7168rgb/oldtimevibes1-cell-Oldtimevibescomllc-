Glossary of Terms

Action
The distance between the strings and the fretboard. High action makes playing harder; low action is faster but may buzz.
Intonation
The accuracy of pitch at every fret. If intonation is off, the guitar will sound out of tune higher up the neck.
Relic
A process of artificially aging a guitar to give it a vintage, worn-in look and feel.
Truss Rod
A metal rod inside the neck used to adjust the curvature (relief) to counteract string tension.
